🕊️ Solo Bird – Delete Your Account

We respect your privacy and give you full control of your game data. Follow these steps to permanently delete your Solo Bird account.

🪶 Step-by-Step Guide

  • Open the Solo Bird game on your device.
  • Go to the Settings menu.
  • Tap on “Delete Account”.
  • A confirmation window will appear — tap Yes to confirm.
  • You will be logged out instantly, and your data will be deleted permanently.

🕒 What Happens Next

  • Your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.
  • You won’t be able to log in using the same credentials again.
  • You can still create a new guest or registered account if you wish to play again.
